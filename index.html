Kintsugi Backup

A personal backup utility that uses Google Drive as a destination for encrypted recovery archives.

Purpose

This application is operated for personal use. It connects to a Google Drive account authorized by its owner and stores backup archives produced by local backup software.

Backup content is encrypted locally before being uploaded. The Google Drive integration uses access limited to files created or opened by this application, subject to Google's granted permissions.

Access and control

The Google account owner grants access through Google's OAuth consent process and can revoke that access through their Google Account security settings. This website does not provide a public account registration or upload service.
